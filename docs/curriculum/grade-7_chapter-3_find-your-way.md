### מהו Imperatives give directions?

An imperative commonly begins with the base form of a verb. 'Go north.' 'Turn right.' 'Continue along Pine Street.' The person receiving the direction is understood, so the subject you is usually not written. 'Turns right' is not the ordinary imperative form. Please can make a direction sound more polite: 'Please continue straight'.

## Places and Directions

Directions describe a route from a starting point to a destination. They combine places, movement words, and an order of steps. A place description says where something is; a direction says how to reach it.

תיאור מקום אומר איפה הוא נמצא, והוראות הגעה מסבירות איך להגיע אליו. כדי להבין מסלול צריך לדעת מהיכן מתחילים, לאן מגיעים ובאיזה סדר פועלים.

## Original dialogue: The way to the park

Visitor: Excuse me. Where is the park? Resident: It is on Pine Street, after the café. Visitor: How do I get there from the library? Resident: Go north along Oak Road to the junction. Turn right onto Pine Street. Go past the café. The park is next. Visitor: Right at the junction, then past the café? Resident: That's right. The visitor repeats the turning point and landmark to check the route.

## illustration

Go north to the junction, turn right, and go past the café to reach the park.

## Movement words

go straight / turn

Go straight means continue without changing direction at that point. Turn means change direction. A turn needs a direction or destination to be useful.

along / onto

Along Oak Road follows that road. Onto Pine Street identifies the road reached by the turn.

past / towards

Go past the café means continue beyond it. Go towards the café means move in its direction; it does not necessarily mean pass it.

junction / destination

A junction is where roads meet. A destination is the place the route is intended to reach. Here the destination is the park.

## Imperatives give directions

An imperative commonly begins with the base form of a verb.

'Go north.' 'Turn right.' 'Continue along Pine Street.' The person receiving the direction is understood, so the subject you is usually not written. 'Turns right' is not the ordinary imperative form.

Please can make a direction sound more polite: 'Please continue straight'.

Position is different from movement

Next to, opposite, and between describe relationships between places.

'The café is next to the park' describes nearby places. 'Go past the café' describes movement. The verb is tells a location; go introduces an action along the route.

A reader needs both kinds of information to distinguish a landmark from the destination.

## Left and right depend on the direction faced

A person moving north turns right to go east and left to go west. A person moving south turns right to go west. On the example route, the visitor is moving north before turning right. Without that starting direction, right alone would not identify east.

עזרה בעברית

ימין ושמאל נקבעים לפי כיוון ההליכה, ולא רק לפי דף המפה. לפני פנייה בדקו לאיזה כיוון האדם פונה; ״ימינה״ יכולה להוביל למזרח או למערב במצבים שונים.

## Order and landmarks

First

First identifies the initial step: 'First, go north from the library.' A starting point makes the instruction meaningful.

Next

Next introduces the following step: 'Next, turn right at the junction.' It links the turn to the route already begun.

Then

Then continues the sequence: 'Then, go past the café.' The café confirms progress along Pine Street.

Finally

Finally marks the last step or arrival: 'Finally, the park is after the café.' It closes the ordered description.

עזרה בעברית

מילות הרצף מחברות את שלבי המסלול. בכל שלב קראו גם את הפעולה וגם את המקום שבו מבצעים אותה, כדי לא לפנות מוקדם או מאוחר מדי.

## An accurate short version

'From the library: north to the junction, right onto Pine Street, past the café to the park.' This compact version keeps the start, turn, road, landmark, and destination. 'Turn right near a building' removes the details that distinguish this route from others.

עזרה בעברית

גרסה מקוצרת של מסלול עדיין צריכה לשמור את נקודת המוצא, הפנייה, הרחוב וציון הדרך. ניסוח כללי כמו ״ליד בניין״ אינו מספיק כשהוא מתאים למקומות רבים.
