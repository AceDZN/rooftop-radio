# Rooftop Radio curriculum mapping

Audience: Hebrew-speaking Israeli grade-7 learners, with optional use by grade-8 learners revisiting the material. Scope is written directions and comparisons applied during movement. Spoken English, productive grammar and learning gains are not assessed.

Saved source evidence is in `docs/curriculum/`. All English fixtures below are original game writing, not Workbook quotations. The 27 October redesign removes the former café/opposite puzzle and keeps landmark identities fixed.

| Delivery | Saved Workbook source | Original game English | Required physical interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 First signal | [Find your way](https://english.workbook.co.il/grade-7/chapter-3/find-your-way) | Deliver the signal to the radio next to the greenhouse. | Reach the radio visibly beside the glasshouse. Jump/glide coaching is separate; the broad alternate route remains valid. |
| 2 Past and sequence | [Find your way](https://english.workbook.co.il/grade-7/chapter-3/find-your-way) | First, tune the greenhouse radio. Go past the greenhouse to the bell tower and tune its radio. | Tune in order and physically pass beyond the greenhouse. The HUD shows the current stage. |

| 4 A locally compared feature | [Choose a route](https://english.workbook.co.il/grade-7/chapter-3/read-connect-choose-route), [Compare two places](https://english.workbook.co.il/grade-7/chapter-3/compare-two-places) | The glass parcel needs space. | 5 Changed order and approach | [Find your way](https://english.workbook.co.il/grade-7/chapter-3/find-your-way), [Choose a route](https://english.workbook.co.il/grade-7/chapter-3/read-connect-choose-route) | First, tune the bell-tower radio, then the greenhouse radio. Radio Station stays west and the café stays east. The context changes through order and approach, without renaming scenery. |

The comparison sources distinguish measured features from preference and do not imply that every wider/longer path is universally preferable. The redesigned sentence explicitly limits its comparison to two visible crossings and connects width to the stated parcel need. `Opposite` is not assessed by this version.

Hebrew help/vocabulary are on demand. Task English is explicitly LTR. Reading, help, audio settings and vocabulary pause movement time. Vocabulary, hints, retries and first decisions are recorded locally without identities or remote analytics. The final transfer permits vocabulary but has no solution hint. Signal notes, air bursts and flow scores measure movement/play, separately from English decisions and assistance.

Independent semantic fixtures verify static names, staged English, ordered actions, fresh passage and compass/turn truth. Input-only simulation and actual keyboard/touch browser routes verify the physical consequences. These checks establish behavior and solvability, not learner comprehension or enjoyment. See `PLAYTEST.md`, `VISUAL_REVIEW.md`, and `docs/review/REDESIGN.md` for the bounded evidence.
